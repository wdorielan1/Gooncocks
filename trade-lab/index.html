    Scouting Report

    How to read this

    Grade
    How a position compares with the average team in the league. A is well above average, C is about average, F is well below. The scale runs A+ A A− B+ B C C− D+ D D− F.
    Pts/wk
    What the starters at that position score per week, added together (for example, 3 starting WRs combined), with the league average underneath.
    Rank
    Where the team stands in the league at that position. 1st is the best.

    Using it for trades: low grades (D, F) are what to trade for. Find teams with A’s there in the Matchmaker. A’s are your trade bait, since you can spare a player there.
